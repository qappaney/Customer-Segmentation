# Customer Segmentation with KMeans Clustering

This project uses KMeans clustering to segment customers based on their annual income and spending score. The dataset consists of demographic and behavior data, including customer age, income, and spending scores. By applying KMeans, the goal is to group customers into distinct clusters based on their income and spending patterns. This can help businesses target their marketing strategies and personalize offerings for different customer segments.

## Project Description

### Overview
The dataset consists of customers' personal and behavioral data. The KMeans clustering algorithm is applied to identify distinct customer segments based on the following features:
- **Age**: The customer's age.
- **Income**: Annual income in thousands of dollars.
- **Spending Score**: A score (from 1 to 100) assigned to customers based on their purchasing behavior.

The project involves:
- Cleaning and preprocessing the data.
- Standardizing features.
- Applying KMeans clustering.
- Visualizing customer segments.

### Objectives
- Segment customers into groups based on their spending habits and income.
- Visualize the clusters to identify patterns.
- Provide business insights into customer behavior.
